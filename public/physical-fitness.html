What is the PRT?

A requirement in the USNSCC is for cadets to take the PRT (Physical Readiness Test) two times a year, once in the spring and once in the winter. Cadets must pass their PRT to stay in the program and attend summer/winter training.

1-Mile Run

Timed completing a 1-mile distance.

Pushups

Maximum reps in 1 minute.

Plank

Maximum hold time (capped at 3:30).

Scoring Levels

The physical standards vary by age and are categorized into 5 levels. The overall fitness level will be determined by the lowest score achieved across all three physical challenges.

L1

Failed / Expired

Represents a cadet who hasn't met all three requirements for Level 2 or higher, or whose PRT score is older than 6 months.

L2

Progressing

A transition phase for newer cadets. To attend Recruit Training (RT) and advance to Seaman Apprentice (SA), cadets only need a Level 2 score.

L3

Satisfactory

Required to progress to Seaman (SN) or higher, and required to attend advanced trainings.

L4+

Good & Excellent

Exceptional physical fitness. Same core privileges as Level 3, but highly distinguished.

The Pushup Requirements

Strict Form Enforced

  • Cadets must place hands directly beneath shoulders or slightly wider. Wide pushups will not be counted.
  • The body must be completely flat (head, shoulders, back, and hip aligned). Dipping or raising will invalidate the rep.
  • Arms must BREAK NINETY DEGREES. No exceptions.
  • If any one of your knees touches the ground, your pushup count ends immediately.
